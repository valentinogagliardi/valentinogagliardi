## Two complementary roles

🟢 **As a Trainer**, I teach development teams how to write sustainable, accessible, and performant code through practical training in web sustainability, software testing, and modern web standards.

🟢 **As a Tech Lead**, I lead architectural refactoring and modernization efforts that reduce technical debt, improve performance, and balance innovation with real-world budgets.

## Teaching & community

- **2,000+ hours of training delivered** to tech companies and through accredited training providers across Italy.
- **Published author** of *Decoupled Django* (Apress) and *Refactoring to React Hooks* (Manning).
- **Active speaker** in the developer community, with talks at PyCon, Codemotion, and meetups.
- **Open source contributor** and **Django Software Foundation member**.

## How I help teams as a Tech Lead

- Build accessible, ethical, and sustainable digital products that put people first.
- Modernize software architectures and development workflows to improve team productivity.
- Find pragmatic solutions to complex technical problems while avoiding over-engineering.
- Lead strategic refactoring and migrations to modern technologies while keeping the business running.
- Balance innovation and budget by choosing technologies that deliver real value.

**The results:** faster teams, higher-quality code, architectures that remain sustainable over time, and technical decisions aligned with budgets and business goals.
